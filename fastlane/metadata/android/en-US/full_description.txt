TodUno brings four essential tools into one Android application.

LUNAR CALENDAR
Explore the Moon phases for every day of the month, upcoming lunar events, personal notes and reminders.

MOON OBSERVATORY
Explore the illuminated Moon, lunar age, distance, orbital data, phase predictions and eclipses. Select the Northern or Southern Hemisphere.

WORLD CLOCK AND TIME TOOLS
Customize world clocks and time zones, view astronomical time, and use the stopwatch and countdown timer.

SCIENTIFIC CALCULATOR
Calculate trigonometric functions, logarithms, powers, roots and expressions. Keep a local calculation history.

Core functions work offline and do not require an account. Notes, preferences and calculation history remain on the device, subject to Android system backup settings.
